Platform logic

Atlas breadth, analytical depth, evolutionary context, and practical actionability

UGO-Omics is designed as a discovery platform rather than a static file collection. It connects underground-organ resources to cross-species modules, phylogenetic interpretation, benchmark-supported discovery, and practical candidate-family prioritization.

Atlas

51-species underground-organ atlas

Broad resource coverage across rhizomes, tubers, storage roots, corm-like systems, root crowns, and related underground organs.

Backbone

11-species analytical backbone

A curated high-confidence panel for orthogroup transfer, candidate-module analysis, and comparative interpretation.

Evolution

BUSCO phylogenetic context

An independent evolutionary scaffold for interpreting recurrent module signals across phylogenetically divergent species.

Gene-list-to-UGO

Map DEG, QTL, GWAS, or candidate gene lists to UGO-Omics orthogroups, candidate modules, scores, and validation-priority annotations.

Use this workflow when you start from a list of gene or protein IDs rather than a sequence. Compact mode maps directly to prioritized UGO candidate modules. Full mode can also use the complete sequence-to-orthogroup lookup from the release archive.

Paste a DEG, QTL, GWAS, or candidate gene list, generate the local command, and inspect a ranked module-summary output. The browser prepares and explains the workflow; the mapping script runs locally or on HPC.

About UGO-Omics

UGO-Omics is a candidate discovery platform for underground-organ and storage-system biology. It connects cross-species omics resources, underground-organ modules, known-gene anchors, and actionability-oriented candidate prioritization.

UGO-Omics outputs are designed for candidate prioritization and hypothesis generation. Candidate traceability and validation-priority scores do not constitute functional validation.
UGO-Omics v1.8 application-oriented static platform. Outputs are candidate-prioritization resources, not functional validation.

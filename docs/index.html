UGO-Omics

Application-oriented candidate discovery platform for underground-organ biology

Start with your biological question. UGO-Omics helps users check whether a gene belongs to an underground-organ candidate module, find candidate modules for a target organ or species, and prioritize validation-ready candidates for functional follow-up.

11-species high-confidence transfer backbone 51-species resource layer 8/8 candidates remapped 7/8 traceable in both new species Application-first interface

Start with your question

UGO-Omics is organized around four practical discovery workflows.

🔎

I have a gene

Search a gene ID, orthogroup, gene family, or annotation keyword to see whether it belongs to a UGO candidate module.

🌿

I study an organ

Select rhizome, tuber, storage root, bulb, corm, root crown, or stolon to identify top candidate modules.

🧬

I study a species

Choose a species and view UGO candidate modules with homologous members in that species.

✅

I want validation targets

Get a prioritized shortlist of candidate modules with suggested validation systems and assays.

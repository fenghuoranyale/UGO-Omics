# Figure 8. Conserved candidate modules discovered by UGO-Omics across underground-organ systems

UGO-Omics v1.9 uses the 11-species high-confidence transfer backbone and the v1.8 application layer to prioritize biologically interpretable candidate modules across underground-organ systems. Five discovery cases are shown: cell-wall remodeling, meristem/regrowth, stress resilience, hormone-related regulation, and developmental transcription-factor modules. For each module, UGO-Omics integrates UGO score, orthogroup-level traceability, species representation, new-species support in Panicum virgatum and Dioscorea rotundata, organ-context matching, and validation-priority ranking. These results represent candidate-prioritization and hypothesis-generation outputs, not functional validation.

Core evidence chain

1. Cross-species backbone

Six-species orthogroup framework links major underground-organ systems.

2. Rhizome validation

Lotus and ginger provide independent rhizome-bearing systems for validating candidate orthogroups.

3. Candidate hierarchy

v0.5 candidates are mapped to v0.6 orthogroups and re-ranked by new support and benchmark recovery.

4. Benchmark recovery

Conservative recovery detects expected modules: cell wall, developmental TFs, hormone, stress, storage, and regrowth.
